Rain Should Never Stop You.

VARSA is a shared-umbrella network for Indian cities. Pick one up at a public station when the sky opens, drop it at any other station when you're dry.

Three steps from drizzle to dry

No bookings, no carrying one around "just in case".

  1. Step 1

    Find a nearby station

    Open the station finder to see the closest VARSA racks and how many umbrellas each has right now.

  2. Step 2

    Scan the QR and borrow

    Scan the code on the station, and the rack releases one umbrella. Try the simulated scan in the finder below.

  3. Step 3

    Return at any station

    Finished with it? Slide it into a free slot at any participating VARSA station, not just where you started.

Why VARSA

Convenient

No umbrella in your bag on dry days, and one within a short walk when it pours.

Affordable

Pay only for the time you use it, instead of buying an umbrella that gets lost or broken. Pricing will be announced at launch.

Sustainable

One shared umbrella replaces many cheap single-season ones that end up in landfill.

Accessible

Stations sit at public places like metro stops and campuses, so rain cover isn't limited to those who can plan ahead.

Our mission

Everyone who travels through an Indian city in monsoon should be able to stay dry, whether or not they remembered an umbrella that morning.

Japan showed that umbrella sharing works in crowded, rainy cities. VARSA adapts the idea for India: students heading to class, office workers on the commute, and visitors who didn't pack for the weather. We're an early-stage idea, and this site is a preview of the experience we want to build.
